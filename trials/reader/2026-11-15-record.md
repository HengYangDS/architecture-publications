# Reader experiment: record

Runs of 2026-11-14 22:28 to 2026-11-15 00:03 CST under [the protocol](protocol.md), which stood here at commit 57b2e04
before the first run. Deviations are listed at the end.

## What was run

Nineteen items: thirteen on ETHOS at pin 999784081fa3 and six on AIGW CLI at v0.6.1 (abf05858f409); `aigw-04` was
the pilot item and is not part of the experiment. Each item gives the reason of a real change archived after the
pin; the subject names the paths it expects the change to touch. Three arms decide, each run three times on every
item, 171 runs:

| Arm | The subject's room holds                                                                          |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A   | the repository at the pin                                                                         |
| B   | the repository, and the work's model and guide text                                               |
| D   | the repository, and one hand-written page of constraints, flows and decisions (106 and 108 lines) |

Every subject was a fresh headless session of the model `claude-opus-5-5`, with reading tools only, told it had 25
tool calls, and void past 30 calls, one US dollar or fifteen minutes.

## What this does and does not show

- A subject that reads the work's model and guide text beside the repository names the paths of a real later change
  better than one that reads the repository alone, at about one and a half times the tokens. The stop line the
  design wrote for this experiment is not reached.
- One hand-written page of constraints, flows and decisions did not help with this task. The design's judgement
  that such content is worth more to a reader than structure is not supported here; the task asked where a change
  lands, which structure answers, and no task asked whether a change is allowed, which constraints would answer.
- Nineteen items, two works by one owner, one model as every subject, one task. AIGW CLI has six items where the
  protocol asked for eight. The pass line of 0.15 was the committee's and had no precedent.
- Arm C, the plates as images, is record-only; see below.

## Deviations

- 2026-11-14 22:34 CST. `run-reader.py` was corrected after registration (commit 57b2e04 names the bytes that
  failed). Its building of arm B's room called `git archive` with a path form Git refuses, so arm B could not start
  for either repository; no arm B subject had run. The correction changes room building only: the archive is taken
  from the checkout's top level, and a room is built under a temporary name and renamed, so a failed build cannot
  leave a room without its work. The twelve arm A answers recorded before the correction (AIGW CLI's six and the
  first of ETHOS's) were made by the registered bytes; the prompt, the tools, the budget and the recording are
  unchanged. The corrected script's SHA-256 is recorded with the results.
- 2026-11-14 22:28 CST. A first start of run 1 ran nothing: the shell wrapper passed the three arms as one word.
  No subject was started.
- 2026-11-14 23:46 CST. Every void run of the deciding arms but two was a subject that spent its one-dollar budget
  before it answered ("no parsable answer": 16 of 18 after the first pass; one made more than 30 tool calls, one
  answered in no readable form). Such voids are not chance: they fall on the arm with more to read (B 11, D 5, A 2)
  and on the items with the most answer paths. The registered scorer leaves void runs out of a run's mean, and the
  protocol does not say what a run that is void twice counts as. The record therefore gives the registered score
  beside two others computed by a second script, written after the first pass and before the reruns: void
  runs counted as recall 0, and only the items valid in every arm and run. The decision is read from the registered
  score; the other two say how far it depends on the voids.
- 2026-11-14 23:48 CST. The one rerun the protocol gives a void run was made for all eighteen after the three runs
  had ended, not after each run. The first pass's void records are kept in the runs file, marked as run again. Four runs were void again,
  all in arm B on ETHOS.

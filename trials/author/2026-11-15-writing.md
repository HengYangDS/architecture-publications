  host's load was between 16 and 22 during the run, and the two models differ from the one that passes, so either the
  layout search on those models or the host could be the cause. The models of those two moments were not kept.

## Follow-ups this record raises

- `archpub check` has no bound on its own time: a queue item or a maintenance Change should give the layout search
  one and say what it tried when it refuses.
- `plate_unfittable` cost the author more than the content did. This is evidence for the queue items `one-anatomy`
  and `blocks-by-question`, which remove the choices the holder spent its time on.
- The trial's wrapper should keep every model the holder checks, so that a slow check can be repeated.

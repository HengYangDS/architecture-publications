# Author's timing, page only: protocol

Version 1, 2026-11-15, written after the writing part of the author's timing trial ([record](2026-11-15-writing.md)) and
before this run. That part reached its ninety-minute limit with nothing delivered, and about fifty-five of its
minutes went to making one view fit a plate of the print edition. A dissent from outside the committee, kept with
Architecture Publisher's working records, asked in its second point for the cost of the publisher's own plate
engine to be measured early. This run measures one side of it: what the same task costs when no plate has to fit.

## What differs from the writing part, and what does not

Same subject (`obra/episodic-memory`), same pin (f5e85f3983a2e6d6d0d36052de04a94d499d83c3), same released archive
(Architecture Publisher 0.14.0), same kind of holder (one fresh headless agent, no memory, no hook, no Skill of the
host), same tools and permissions, same limits (ninety minutes, thirty US dollars by the agent's own count), same
wrapper and the same things recorded.

One sentence of the instructions differs: the holder stops when `archpub build --media page` has delivered the page
into `delivery/`, and is told the print edition is left out of this trial. The page is the work's guide, set by
Archify; the plates of the edition are laid out by the publisher's own engine.

The room is new, and the writing part's room is made unreadable for the length of
the run, so that the holder cannot come upon the earlier work. The wrapper also lists every path outside the room
that a tool call names.

## Read as

The minutes to a delivered page, beside the writing part's ninety minutes without a delivery. Not a rate: one
repository, one holder, one run on each side, and the two sides differ in more than the engine, since the page-only
holder also skips the edition's key lines, overview and images. A page delivered in well under the limit says the
plate engine is where an author's time goes; a page not delivered either says the cost lies elsewhere.

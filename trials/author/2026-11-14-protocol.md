# Author's timing: protocol

Version 1, 2026-11-14, written before the run. What it measures: what it costs an agent that has never seen a
repository to write a work on it from the bundled Skill alone, and to move that work to a later commit.

## Subject

`obra/episodic-memory`, a repository that is not the owner's, from the local clone as it stands (212 tracked
files). The work is not published and nothing is sent anywhere.

- Writing: the work is pinned at the commit thirty first-parent commits before the clone's head.
- Re-pinning: the same work is moved to the clone's head.

## Holder

One headless agent per part, with no memory, no hook and no Skill of the host. Its room, outside the home
directory, holds a local clone of the repository, the released Architecture Publisher 0.14.0 archive, and nothing
else. It may read and write inside the room and run two programs: the archive's `archpub`, and `git` on the clone
for reading. Its instructions are four sentences: what the room holds, to run `archpub skill` and follow the Skill
it prints, the commit to pin, and to stop when `archpub build` has delivered the edition and the page; it is told
not to accept the delivery, since an author does not review its own work.

The re-pinning holder is a fresh agent that gets the room as the writer left it.

## Recorded

For each part: wall-clock minutes, tokens and cost as the agent reports them, the number of `archpub check` and
`archpub build` runs and how many of them were refused, each refusal's code, and whether the part ended with a
delivered edition and page. For the written work: its components, relations, views and cited files, and what
`archpub check` reports of coverage. For the re-pinning: the worklist `archpub check --against` gave (citations
the same, shifted, touched, moved, gone) and how many the holder had to reread.

## Limits

One repository, one holder, one run: a first number, not a rate. Limits per part: ninety minutes and thirty US
dollars by the agent's own count; a part that reaches either ends there and is recorded as unfinished. No pass
line: the result is reported, and it is the first datum on whether an agent can draft a first edition in minutes.

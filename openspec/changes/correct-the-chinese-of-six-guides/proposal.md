## Why

A work's page in a browser is built from its guide: `guide.archify.md` and the figure files beside it. The Change
`correct-the-chinese-of-six-works` corrected each work's term table, its Chinese model and its overview, and left
the guides as they were: a Chinese page still called ETHOS's proof 证明 and its Attestation 核验记录, a provider's
catalog 目录 and Film Kit's receipts 回执, beside an edition that no longer did. The owner asked on 2026-11-17 for
standard, idiomatic Chinese fit for what a work speaks of, and for wording that is weighed.

A reader other than the writer then read each Chinese guide against its English and the corrected table: 252
findings in six guides, 92 of them on a table's changed rendering still standing, the rest wrong terms, one thing
named two ways, word-for-word phrasing and sentences that have to be read twice.

## What Changes

- Each work's Chinese guide follows its corrected term table and its Chinese model, in the article and in the
  labels and texts of its figures: 421 lines in 35 files of six works. A word that is also plain Chinese keeps its
  plain sense where it has it: 证明 as "to show" stays in ETHOS and DDWG.
- Six rows of four term tables follow what the readings settled: DDWG's release declaration is 发布配置, as
  ETHOS's is; Film Kit's bound is 限值, as its model says, and its recognition proxy 只作旁证的识别结果; Architecture
  Publisher's lock is 锁文件; two rows that OpenAI Responses Proxy's table repeated are removed.
- One figure of Architecture Publisher's Chinese guide names its locale `zh-CN`, as the other eight do.
- No id, anchor, source mark, citation, number, identifier, command or English text changes, and no figure's
  structure.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

`publications/<work>/zh/guide/` of the six works; `publications/<work>/concepts.md` of four; `CHANGELOG.md`. The
English guides keep their words: the faults the readers found in them are listed in the design.

## Why

On 9 November the owner read the Chinese editions and pages of the six works and found much of their wording literal
and machine-like, unpleasant to read, and chose seven renderings from their review list, each as suggested there.

## What Changes

- Each translation is polished sentence by sentence against its English original, so that it reads as natural written
  Chinese: no English word order, no calques a reader cannot decode, no card details that read as translated
  key–value pairs. Every fact, qualifier, identifier and citation is kept.
- ETHOS: provenance is 出处, not 来源, as each claim's 出处 already reads; profile is 配置档, not 配置, so it no longer
  reads as configuration (配置文件).
- AIGW: fails closed is 出错即拒绝, not the literal 失败关闭.
- Architecture Publisher: the house class is 版式类, not 本社版式, as a document class is a 文档类, and its faces,
  tokens and rules follow (版式字体, 版式设计令牌, 版式规则); a pinned revision is 固定的修订 and a re-pin 重新固定,
  not 钉定修订 and 重新钉定; the usage error of exit status 2 is 用法错误, not 误用.
- filmkit: supply is 供应, not 供给.
- Each change is made in the work's model, overview, guide and term table alike, so the edition and the page agree.

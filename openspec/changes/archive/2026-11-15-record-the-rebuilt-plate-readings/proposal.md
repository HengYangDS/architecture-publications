## Why

sentence promises the record of the readings "in a dated file beside this one". The readings were taken on
2026-11-15. They met the lines of the cited-file count and of the unchanged items and missed the hub's line by one
answer, 34 of 36 against 35. By the registered rule the row that draws lines into a view's hub heavy would have left
Architecture Publisher's table of lines. The result was put to the owner, who ruled that the row stays. A reader of
the pass line finds none of this here.

The same morning a holder other than the drawing's author read four rebuilt plates against a list written before it
saw a plate. That reading has no record here either.

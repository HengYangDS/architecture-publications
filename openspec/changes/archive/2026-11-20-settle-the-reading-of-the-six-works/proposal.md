## Why

On 2026-11-20 four readers other than the writer read the twelve editions whole, as the publisher's development
head builds them: two read the Chinese and two the English. They returned 118 findings; about ninety-six are of
the works' own words. Some are of fact. One work calls the operations of its two entries the same in one place
and names five of them in another; one counts seven topics under a charter that is itself one of the seven; one
says a sent request is never resent beside a row that sends the same bytes once more; one orders the saving of a
configuration after the writing of the files it configures, where the source saves first. Others are of
reading: in all six works the first sentence under "What it leaves out" says what is listed and not what is left
out; a part's name stands in a sentence as an ordinary verb or noun; a plate's question repeats its title; one
thing has two names.

A work says what its repository at the pin does, says it the same in both languages, and reads as its language.

## What Changes

- Each statement of fact that two places worded differently is settled against the described repository at the
  work's pin, in both languages.
- The first limit of every work states a bound: a component lists only the files under the paths it claims, and
  a relation only the modules or files that make it.
- A part's name that would read as ordinary words is marked as a name where it stands in a sentence, and takes
  a singular verb.
- A plate's question asks what its title leaves open, and no work's claim is a plate's title.
- One thing has one name within a work, and each work's `concepts.md` follows: among them the kind "client
  adapter" and the part "Program entry" of the AIGW Client work, the proxy's 世代选择器, and "release" for what
  the guidelines publish, where "edition" also named this atlas.
- A limit no longer points to notes or details the edition does not hold.

No pin moves, no part, relation or view is added or removed, and no citation changes.

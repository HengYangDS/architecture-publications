## Context

See [proposal.md](proposal.md). The readers' reports, the editors' and the reviewers' records of every changed
value, and the comparison of each work against the publications' development head are kept with the maintainer's
working records and are not tracked files; this Change holds their result.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Twelve editions whose words a reader other than the writer confirms, in both languages.

**Non-Goals:**

- The words Architecture Publisher itself prints on every edition (its headings, the reading note, a key's
  label): a Change of that product.
- The guides' prose beyond the sentences the findings touch.

## Decisions

1. A fact is settled at the pin, never by the better sentence.
   - Each work had an editor, who took the findings of both languages and read the described repository at the
     work's pin for every statement two places gave differently, and a reviewer, who confirmed each changed fact
     at the pin again and restored to the former wording what could not be confirmed.
2. The first limit is worded as a bound and stays under "What it leaves out".
   - The readers found the sentence out of place under that heading. Worded with "only", it says what a card and
     a relation do not list, which is what the heading promises; the product's label is not changed for it.
3. A part's name is marked only where it would read as ordinary words.
   - In Chinese with quotation marks, in English with the card's capitals. A name that already reads as a name
     stands bare, so that a sentence with three parts is not a row of quotation marks.
4. A citation's title is the heading of what it cites.
   - Two editors had reworded the titles of citations to the works' new terms; a citation names its source's own
     heading at the pin, and the four were restored.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Some texts are longer than they were, where a fact needed the words. → All twelve editions were built from
  these files and every page holds its text.
- The readings were made by agents, none by a person. → Three passes by different readers: the first reading,
  the reviewers' reading of every change, and a reading of the built pages.

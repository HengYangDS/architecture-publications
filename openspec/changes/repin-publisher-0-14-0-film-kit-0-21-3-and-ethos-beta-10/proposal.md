## Why

A work follows its repository's latest release. Architecture Publisher has released 0.14.0, Film Kit 0.21.3 and
ETHOS 0.2.0-beta.10, and their works still pin v0.13.1, v0.21.2 and v0.2.0-beta.9. The publisher now sets a part's
details as a justified paragraph in an edition written without spaces between words, measures its house faces with
their pair kerning, and sets a plate's key by its baseline with every mark centred on its label; its work says none
of it. ETHOS gives the files of its canonical-text format an owner, holds every authored Markdown list to one
blank-line rule, requires a docstring in each package marker, and names the paradigm repair as the next action of a
refusal it can repair; its work describes the rules before these. Film Kit's release moves its supply and its
engine package's version, which the work states.

## What Changes

- Architecture Publisher's work pins v0.14.0, Film Kit's v0.21.3 and ETHOS's v0.2.0-beta.10, in both languages,
  with every shifted citation moved and every touched one reread by the re-pin worklist.
- Each work says what its release adds that a reader of the work would look for, and its history ends at the last
  release up to the pin that changed what the system does for its users.
- Each work's README, term table and scope name the new pin, and `CHANGELOG.md` records the moves.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

None. `archpub check --repo` verifies every citation at the new pins, and the input check already requires a
README and term table that name each work's pinned revision.

## Impact

`publications/architecture-publisher/`, `publications/filmkit/` and `publications/ethos/`: the models, overviews,
guides, READMEs and term tables; `CHANGELOG.md`. The described repositories are unchanged. The works on AIGW and
the Responses proxy keep their pins, as the last re-pin left them: each waits for its repository's history rebuild,
which replaces the commits a work binds. The work on the work guidelines pins its latest release.

## Observations

- Most entries bundle two or three sub-questions, and the three states have no "partly answered". Three entries
  are CITED on one half only. `verification-and-release` is the weakest: under a strict reading the counts
  become CITED 11, NOT APPLICABLE 0, SILENT 2.
- Name collision is the main trap for this repository. The engine's own subject is verification, review,
  delivery, release and concepts of a film. C: `verification`, C: `delivery`, C: `word-checks` and V:
  `acceptance` look like answers to `verification-and-release` and `terms`, but they describe what the engine
  does to a film, not how the repository itself is verified, released or worded. I did not count them.
- `overview.typ` carries no source citation on any element. Its masthead says the page is written from 119
  files, each cited by SHA-256, but no element names the file behind it. Under the rule for text, no overview
  statement counts as evidence on its own; every overview statement I could have used repeats a cited guide
  statement.
- One guide section answers several entries loosely: `#p-context` is the main evidence for positioning,
  context-and-goals, data-and-interfaces and stack-and-deployment, and `#p-maturity` for
  constraints-and-invariants, verification-and-release, decisions-and-risks and the direction half of
  changes-and-direction. V: `overview` could be read as answering both top-level-design and mechanisms; the
  model's own `limits` rules out the second reading.
- No entry is answered by a view alone. Views carry a question and members but no files; components and
  relations carry the evidence. `mechanisms` is answered only by the guide.
- Process content the repository holds and the work omits: `CONTRIBUTING.md` (prepare, change, verify, commit,
  release), `system/gates.toml` (three proof gates), `.ethos/release.toml` (two publication peers, protected
  `v*` tags), the SemVer rule that the declaration format may change in a minor release before 1.0.0.
- Constraint and decision content the repository holds and the work omits: `openspec/specs/` (17 capability
  specifications, 134 requirement headings) and 38 archived Changes whose `design.md` files have decisions and
  risks sections. `coverage.exclude` names `openspec/` as accepted requirements and change history, so the work
  knows of this content and leaves it out.
- `constraints-and-invariants` is cited mostly by run-time rules that the engine enforces on a film. The
  question's second half, what a change must never break, has one sentence, cited to `AGENTS.md`.
- Misfits in the question table for a locally run command-line engine: "how is it kept running" has no object;
  "what data does it keep" fits only as records written into another repository's `build/`. No whole entry is
  NOT APPLICABLE; the misfit is at the level of a clause, which the three states cannot record.
- `terms` shows a gap between a rendering table and a glossary. `concepts.md` has 140 term rows with a file per
  term and would pass a mechanical "file exists" check, yet it states a meaning in a sentence for one term only
  (edition, in its preamble); other rows carry at most a short gloss in parentheses.
